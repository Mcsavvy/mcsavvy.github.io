05Questions

Before you apply

Is it really free? What's the catch?

The page, a domain and 12 months of hosting are free. The trade is the testimonial, permission to share your site, and a small footer credit. You only pay if you later ask for something beyond the one page.

Who owns the domain and the site?

You do. If I register your domain, it's in your business's name, and I hand over access on request. If you use a free subdomain, you can move to your own domain any time.

How are the five businesses chosen?

By fit, not by who applies first. I'm looking for a mix of businesses that are ready to go, so every site can be finished on its day.

What if my content isn't ready in time?

You have 48 hours after selection. If nothing arrives, the slot passes to the next business on the list, so please only apply if you can send it.

What if I need more than one page?

Start with the free page. If you want more pages, a shop or an app afterwards, I'll quote it separately before any work starts.

What if I'm not chosen?

You'll still hear from me on Sunday 18 October, and you'll get first notice when the next batch opens.
